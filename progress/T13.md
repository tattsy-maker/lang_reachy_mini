# T13 — Family feedback round 1 — progress notes

Status: **in progress** — T13.1–T13.6 and T13.9 built and green on the
simulated path (2026-09-02); the in-person checks are open. Scope decisions the
same day: T13.5 quips carry no Skynet/Terminator/uprising allusions; the
T13.6 poster is a physical deliverable and out of the code scope (only
the `record_wish` tool was built); T13.7–T13.9 are nice-to-have.

Source: the recorded family
debrief right after the first in-person rehearsal (three speakers, ~45
minutes at the robot, cloud voice, no booth mic yet). The transcript
was in Russian; this is the digest, then where each point went.

## What the family said

**Hearing and speed**
- Replies felt slow at times (probably the room, not the model — cloud
  TTFB measured 0.6–1.7 s in T8).
- It hears *everyone* in the room, which is unusable at a booth. "The
  mic has to be plugged straight into that computer."

**Identity and memory**
- Face recognition visibly worked ("it knew you when it saw you").
- There is no voice recognition. Idea: store a *voice print* next to the
  face signature, notice when a known face has the wrong voice, and say
  so playfully ("you don't sound like yourself today — suspicious, say
  something else").
- Nobody could say how long the robot keeps believing you are there
  after you leave ("a minute?"). Covering the camera did not seem to end
  the session; the family *wants* "go make tea, keep talking, it does
  not forget you".

**Speech quality**
- In a mixed sentence one language gets the other's accent: English
  words inside a Russian sentence come out with a Russian accent.
  Verdict at the table: "nothing we can do for now — plan the demo
  around it."

**Tutoring**
- Italian "started strangely"; French asked the level right away. The
  tutor must *explicitly* establish the level during enrollment.
- Levels matter because *goals* differ: one person wants only
  conversation, another is preparing for an exam, a third for a job
  interview. The tutor should behave differently per goal (e.g. offer
  synonyms and a more sophisticated register). "User profiles in the
  database."

**Booth and showmanship**
- "Something critical is missing for the Faire": the mic (above), and
  the robot needs jokes with a little edge ("I'll remember you";
  "Albu-Bak" — their pronunciation of "I'll be back").
- A board or poster is needed so the booth is visible from a distance;
  people look from afar before they dare to approach. On it: what it
  can and *especially* what it cannot do yet; diagrams of what is
  happening; a way for visitors to leave wishes ("if you bought this as
  a product, what would you want it to do?").
- The robot must do the standard Reachy Mini tricks — dance, spin.
- It should follow the current speaker with head and body: up, down,
  left, right, wherever you walk.
- The computer hung at the end of the session.
- Next step agreed: run the next iteration, then test again tomorrow
  with a microphone.

## Facts checked while writing the task (so the implementer need not)

- Motion tools today: `move_head`, `turn_body`, `nod`, `shake_head`,
  `wiggle_antennas`, `reset_pose`, `get_robot_status` (voice/agent.py);
  driver procedures: `goto_posture`, `home`, `look_at`, `nod`, `shake`.
  No recorded-move playback anywhere yet.
- The vendor SDK in `.venv` has `RecordedMoves` backed by the HuggingFace
  datasets `pollen-robotics/reachy-mini-dances-library` and
  `pollen-robotics/reachy-mini-emotions-library`, plus
  `look_at_image(u, v)` / `look_at_world(x, y, z)`; `reachy_target.py`
  already uses `look_at_world`.
- `face/recognize.py` only exposes `embed()` (largest face, embedding);
  there is no detection-only call, and the session runner never sees a
  bbox — T13.3 adds both.
- `SessionMachine.on_face` is the only presence input; speech does not
  extend presence today. Default `--absent-secs 60`, `--stable-secs 2`.
- Profile keys: name, target_language, level, embedding, sessions,
  last_seen, tier. No goal. `enroll_new_learner` accepts `level` but the
  stranger briefing says "beginner unless they say otherwise" — that is
  the inconsistency the family saw.
- `embodiment.py` owns pitch and antennas by convention and documents
  that tool calls own yaw; T13.3's tracker must respect that split.
- Face pipeline runs on CPU at ~10 fps capacity (T2), so 2 fps detection
  during conversation costs nothing on the GPU.

Learnings:

1. **`RobotLink.call(name, **args)` and a driver argument called
   `name` collide** — the first `perform` died with "multiple values for
   argument 'name'". The rpc argument is `move`; avoid `name` in any
   future rpc signature.
2. **`UNSURE_BRIEFING` is formatted with `name=` only**, so a shared
   script with a `{languages}` placeholder must have it spelled out
   before being appended there, or `.format` raises `KeyError`.
3. **The fixture clip's face sits off-centre**, which is exactly what
   makes the models-marked tracker test meaningful: it asserts the yaw
   sign matches the face's side of the frame on every command.
4. **A late `FrameHub` reader still gets the final frame once.** Kept:
   an enrollment snapshot after a file source ends is better than none,
   and a live camera never "ends".
5. The T9 enrollment test needed two more scripted answers (level, goal)
   — any future dialog test that enrolls must answer all four questions.

Open (needs the robot and people): the in-person E2E in the task text,
plus the second family session with the booth mic. Watch for, on metal:
the ±35° handoff feeling too eager on a real neck, the attractor
startling people who are just reading the sign, and whether "still
there?" at 40 s is too soon for a learner thinking about an answer.

## 2026-09-02 (evening): T13.9 voice prints

Built on the user's other project's setup: SpeechBrain ECAPA
(`speechbrain/spkrec-ecapa-voxceleb` via `EncoderClassifier`, with the
older import path as fallback). `voice/.venv` already had torch 2.13
(cu130) and torchaudio; `pip install speechbrain` (1.1.1) was the only
addition.

**Measured, this box.** Model load 5.3 s; one 3 s clip **5 ms on
cuda:0, 40 ms on CPU** — the opposite of the face model's story (T2),
so `provider_report()` is part of the API and the gate records it.

**Gate** (`voice/verify_voiceid.py`, report
`tests/reports/verify_voiceid_2026-09-02.*`): 20 clips, five Kokoro
voices × four sentences (`tests/fixtures/voices/`, provenance in the
fixtures README — synthetic on purpose, no human recordings in the
repo).

| pairs | n | min | mean | max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| same speaker | 30 | 0.717 | 0.820 | 0.898 |
| different speaker | 160 | −0.028 | 0.133 | 0.391 |
| hold-out print vs own clip | 20 | 0.786 | 0.874 | 0.929 |
| hold-out print vs other speakers | 80 | −0.012 | 0.145 | 0.330 |

The 0.391 is `af_heart` vs `bf_emma`, two female English Kokoro voices.
Thresholds: **accept ≥ 0.60, reject < 0.45**, ask band between (the
other project's single 0.65 sits just above our accept line). A first
guess of 0.35 for reject failed the gate on that pair — which is what
the gate is for.

**What landed.** `voiceid.py`: `embed_pcm/embed_wav`, `compare`,
`VoiceIdentity` (the fusion policy, pure), `VoiceCollector` (a pipecat
tap; energy-gated because Gemini Live emits no user-speaking frames;
drops the robot's own voice via the bot-speaking frames the output
transport pushes upstream; sits after the user aggregator in cloud mode
and before the STT locally). Store: optional `voice_embedding`.
`tutor_mode.voice_cue` writes the three spoken follow-ups; enrollment
stores the interview's print. Agent: `--no-voice-id`, `--voice-source`
(scripted hearing), `say_cue` (a mid-conversation cue seam that works in
both speech modes — cloud goes through Gemini's own injection path, the
one T12 will need too), voice samples also count as presence for T13.2
(which otherwise never fires in cloud mode: no user-speaking frames).
Session runner resets the voice evidence per visitor. Sign 1 discloses
the voice signature.

**Live** (`tests/run.sh t13 -k voiceid`): enrollment through the
four-question interview with `--voice-source af_heart_1` stored a 192-d
print that matched `af_heart_2` above accept; a seeded Sunita with
`bm_george`'s print, heard as `af_heart`, got "hoy no suenas del todo
como tú misma, ¿me cuentas un poquito más?" then was downgraded to the
ask band.

**Learnings.** (1) Two female Kokoro voices score 0.39 — a reminder that
the reject line needs margin, not a round number. (2) An LLM test that
asserts on a reply *after* a cue must wait for that reply's TTS line
(`also_wait_for`) or the SIGINT beats it. (3) One verified match beats a
later mismatch: a cough or a bystander must not trigger the challenge.

**Open.** Real human calibration: run the gate on the family's
recordings (`--dir`, same layout) at the next session, and watch for
false challenges on a hoarse morning voice. The energy gate's noise
floor is adaptive but untested against a hall's crowd noise.
