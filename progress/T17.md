## Protocol for the next family session

Everything from T15.8 still applies, plus:

1. **Pause mid-sentence for two seconds** while answering. Expect no
   reply until you finish; `turn: first sound` will read about 1.5 s
   higher than today's 2.2 s median. That is the price and it is
   deliberate.
2. **Say one sentence in a silly voice** after enrollment. Expect
   nothing: no "you don't sound like yourself" while your face is in
   frame. Then `grep "voice:" voice/run.log`: the mismatch should be
   logged and overruled, not spoken.
3. **Have someone sit on the second chair and lean in** for five
   seconds mid-lesson. Expect `face check: same` throughout (the
   session's face is found among all faces) and no swap.
4. **Answer the intake as a Russian speaker learning English**: expect
   the questions name, language, *explain in Russian, English or both*,
   level, goal, minutes, and what you want today, one per turn, and
   `tutor: enrolled … (en taught in ru …)` in the log. Then drift into
   Russian mid-lesson: expect one answer in Russian and the next task
   set in English.
5. **Make three mistakes in a row** in the target language. Expect a
   recast after each one, at most one sentence.
6. **Ask for a test.** Expect the word for "blank" spoken in your
   language, three options, one question.
7. **Fool around**: ask it to turn its head five times. Expect two
   complied, then "back to the lesson".
8. **Say goodbye.** Expect one closing question ("what should I do
   better, or what would you want a robot like me to do?") and your
   answer in `booth/feedback.md` whether or not the model called a tool.

## Built (2026-09-05, evening)

All eleven subtasks, on the simulated path, in one sitting. Where the
code lives and what each one enforces:

- **T17.1** `voice/turns.py` (`vad_settings`, `PATIENCE_RULE`);
  `agent.py` passes `GeminiLiveLLMService.Settings(vad=...)` with
  `END_SENSITIVITY_LOW`, `silence_duration_ms` from
  `--turn-patience-ms` (default 1800; `BOOTH_TURN_PATIENCE_MS`) and
  300 ms of prefix padding; the log says `turns: waiting 1800 ms of
  silence before a reply`. `tests/t17/probe_patience.py` streams two
  fixture sentences with a 1.2 s gap to a Live session configured the
  same way and counts turns.
- **T17.2** `voiceid.VoiceIdentity` takes `face_vouches()` (bound to
  `SessionRunner.face_vouches`, which the runner also binds itself the
  first time a face matches); a mismatch while the face vouches returns
  `"overruled"` and is only logged; two overrules or `trust()` (called
  by `confirm_identity`) silence the voice for the session;
  `absorb(learner)` folds the session's samples into the stored print
  (weight 0.5) and `tutor_mode.strengthen_face` folds a confirmed live
  face into the stored face (0.3); `SessionRunner._write_back_face`
  does the same at the end of any session with six or more confident
  matches. Briefing, unsure briefing and the challenge cue say identity
  is the recognizer's call, never a `look` picture's.
- **T17.3** `recognize.analyze_all` / `detect_all` (every face, largest
  first); `SessionRunner.observe(..., faces=)` picks the best match to
  the session face when it clears the accept line, else the largest;
  between recognition passes the box nearest the last match is
  followed; `_session_bbox` feeds the tracker.
- **T17.4** `voice/intake.py` (`Intake`, `SessionPlan`, `time_cue`);
  `intake_answer` tool, `enroll_new_learner` refuses with the missing
  list and the next question, `set_session_plan` for a returning
  learner, `WALKUP_CUE_KNOWN` asks the two session questions, the
  runner injects the wrap-up cue at 80 % of the agreed minutes. The
  profile gains `explain_in` and `corrections`. The interview is seven
  turns (name; language; own language + explain-in; level; goal;
  minutes + today; corrections); answers volunteered in one breath are
  recorded in one turn. `explain_in` is asked but never blocks: the
  first live run (seat-swap clip, 21:10) had Gemini record eight fields
  from one sentence and drop explain-in ("explain in English" became
  native_language only), so enrollment defaults it to "native" and logs
  `explain_in was not asked`; every other field is required.
- **T17.5** `_EXPLAIN_POLICY` (native / target / both), the drift rule,
  "never ask them to say what you just said", `_SCRIPT_RULE` with
  per-language hints (Cyrillic, characters, Devanagari...).
- **T17.6** `tutor_mode.quiz_script` + the `quiz` tool: the gap is
  spoken as the word for "blank" in the language of instruction
  (`BLANK_WORDS`, nine languages), options lettered, one question; a
  `quiz:` log line per item; the briefing allows only formats that work
  by ear.
- **T17.7** `_CORRECTIONS_RULE` per preference (every / blocking /
  end) with the "next rung" line; `HeardLogger` in `agent.py` (one
  `heard:` INFO line per visitor sentence, both pipelines);
  `tests/t17/judge_corrections.py` (Claude Haiku counts mistakes vs
  corrections per session; report in `tests/reports/`).
- **T17.8** `voice/budget.py` (`SightseeingBudget`, two calls of
  `look`/`move_head`/`turn_body` per two minutes during a lesson, reset
  per visitor by the runner); the steer-back, nonsense and notes rules
  in the briefing; the periscope quip in the persona.
- **T17.9** `WISH_QUESTION` asks for feedback or an idea; `record_wish`
  takes `kind` (improve / wish) and writes `booth/feedback.md`
  (`tutor.wishes.record_feedback`); `wish_followup` opens a 90 s window
  and `catch_feedback` (fed by `HeardLogger`) writes the visitor's next
  three-plus words as `[answer]` whether or not the tool is called;
  "Go and practice" only after the answer.
- **T17.10** `voice/pace.py`: `pace:` line per reply
  (`CloudTranscriptLogger`), the beginner guidance says slow and clear,
  and `Stretcher` (streaming WSOLA, numpy only) behind `--speech-rate`
  / `BOOTH_SPEECH_RATE`, off by default. Measured on a 220 Hz test
  tone: factor 1.2 -> 1.19x length, 1.5 -> 1.48x, 0.8 -> 0.80x, level
  within 2 %.
- **T17.11** tool descriptions name the sides from the visitor's view;
  the prompt forbids speaking anything that looks like a tool call;
  the `heard:` line. The double greeting at a reconnect after a swap is
  documented above, not fixed.
