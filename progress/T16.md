# T16 — Any native language — progress notes

Status: **done on the simulated path** (2026-09-04, night). The live
check is the first Russian-speaking visitor who asks for English.

## Why

Everything about the tutor assumed English was the language you are
taught *in*. That was fine for the four Romance languages at the Faire
but wrong for the family, whose debrief was recorded in Russian, and it
made "learn English" impossible: the profile had a target language and
nothing else, so a Russian speaker learning English got English
explanations of English.

## What changed

- `tutor/store.py`: `native_language` in `PROFILE_KEYS`, optional on disk
  (default `en`), lower-cased on load and create.
- `voice/tutor_mode.py`: the briefing gains "Their own language is X:
  every explanation ... is in X"; `_LEVEL_GUIDANCE`, the answer-then-steer
  rule, the garbled-transcript note and the T15.4 task rule use
  `{native}`; `build_unsure_briefing(learner)` asks "is that you?" in the
  candidate's language; `voice_cue` challenge/downgrade say which
  language to ask in; the enrollment script explains that English is a
  valid target and when to ask for the explanation language; the
  stranger greeting says to carry on in whatever language the visitor
  answers in; the booth quip rule says "the student's own language".
  New tool `set_native_language`; `enroll_new_learner` takes
  `native_language`; `confirm_identity` returns it.
- `voice/multilingual.py`: `bilingual_priming(target, native="en")`.
  The seven prompts are all English+X, and the T7 measurements were for
  those pairs, so the pair (ru, en) reuses the ru prompt and a pair with
  no English in it gets no priming rather than an unmeasured one.
- `voice/agent.py`: `--native-language CODE`; in local mode the untagged
  voice is that language (else the `--learner` profile's, else `en`);
  cloud prompt says English can be taught and explanations follow the
  student's language; the tutor-mode log line and the priming log name
  the native language. `voice/session.py` primes on the pair and uses
  `build_unsure_briefing`.
- `start_booth.sh`: `BOOTH_NATIVE_LANGUAGE` (local speech only).

## What the Russian-native briefing says

For Igor, beginner English, native ru:

> Your student is Igor: beginner English, your first session together.
> Their own language is Russian: every explanation, instruction and aside
> is in Russian ... and English is what they practise.
> ... give a brief Russian explanation alongside anything new ...
> Setting a task: say what to express in Russian and have Igor say it in
> English ...

## Decisions

- **Default is English, not "ask everyone".** The interview stays four
  questions for the common visitor; the fifth question is conditional
  (they chose English, or spoke to the robot in something else). Asking
  every Faire visitor "and what language should I explain in?" would
  slow the queue for the majority who answer in English.
- **Cloud mode first.** The family chose the Gemini voice; it reads the
  native language from the profile with no per-launch flag. Local mode
  gets the flag because its voice for untagged text is fixed at launch.
- **No new priming strings.** Priming is a per-pair policy backed by
  measurement (T7); inventing a ru+es prompt without a gate would repeat
  the fr/pt regression T7 found.
