## What the log shows (16 minutes, one launch, three people)

- **The voice print overruled the face, twice.** 18:55:20 and 18:59:16:
  `speaker changed` on two consecutive samples under 0.45 (0.325/0.304,
  then 0.379/0.287) from *the same child*, whose voice had scored 0.683
  and 0.748 minutes earlier. The face re-recognised him at 0.547 five
  seconds after the forced goodbye, which is why "Au revoir!" ran into
  "Bonjour John Maritime!" in one breath and the robot later apologised
  ("je n'ai pas voulu dire au revoir"). Cause: thresholds calibrated on
  synthetic voices (known open item), 1.7–2.5 s samples, a child
  speaking French after an English enrollment, and a rule that let two
  misses end a session.
- **The seat swap.** Tanya enrolled 19:01:50. A voice challenge at
  19:02:41 (0.311) and 19:02:53 (0.364) downgraded her to "is that
  you?"; somebody said yes; `confirm_identity` at 19:03:05. From then
  on **no voice decision was logged** (15 samples): a downgrade sets
  `downgraded=True`, after which `on_sample` returns nothing for the
  rest of the session, and confirming did not reset it. The swap itself
  is visible only as a half-second `presence: voice` / `face` flicker
  at 19:06:23. Mid-session the runner ran `detect`, never `analyze`
  (spec §A: "recognition does not run during conversation"), so the
  new face was just "a face". At 19:07:09 `look` described "a man with
  short blonde hair, grey shirt" and the lesson continued as Dwayne's.
- **Three greetings in fifteen seconds** at the first walk-up (18:51:57
  and 18:52:06), and in the morning run "Hello there. I do not see
  anyone. Is John Marston, is that you?" to an empty chair. pipecat's
  Gemini Live service seeds every new connection with the context's
  system prompt *as a user turn* and, with the default
  `inference_on_context_initialization=True`, has the model answer it.
  That happens at the kick-off reconnect (idle prompt → "Hello there")
  and at each per-visitor reset (briefing → "Hi there! I am Reachy

## What changed (2026-09-04, evening)

- **T15.1** `voice/session.py`: the frame loop is
  `observe(face, now, frame_shape)`; mid-session the loop runs
  `recognize.analyze` every `face_recheck_secs` (2 s) — and at once
  after a face gap ≥ 1 s or a voice doubt — and `_check_identity`
  compares the embedding with the face that started the session
  (`_session_face`). Same face (≥ face ACCEPT 0.45): `_same_face_at`.
  Different face (< face REJECT 0.25) for `swap_secs` (3 s): `face
  swap` → `end_session` (walk-away cue: goodbye + notes) → the watching
  loop starts the newcomer's session from clean `_recent_vectors`.
  `speaker_changed()` now: face seen within `face_vouch_secs` (5 s) →
  ignore and `rearm()` the voice; a face present but unjudged → force a
  recheck and let the face decide; nobody in frame → end as before.
  `BOOTH_NOTE` on every runner prompt. Spec §A rewritten.
- **T15.2** `voice/voiceid.py`: `change_after=4`,
  `decision_min_secs=3.0` (short samples feed the print only; injected
  files are always judged, `secs=None`), `rearm()`, the real-voice
  numbers next to the thresholds. `VoiceCollector.on_speech_end`.
- **T15.3** `voice/tutor_mode.py`: `face_confirms()`;
  `confirm_identity` captures the current face (`current_face` seam,
  default two snapshots from the hub) and refuses below the face reject
  line, telling the model to ask their name and treat them as new;
  success re-arms the voice check.
- **T15.4** `voice/agent.py`: `VISION_FACE_LOOK` via `vision_text()`;
  `tutor_mode._BRIEFING` "Setting a task" rule with `task_language`
  (English below advanced).
- **T15.5** `voice/agent.py`: `inference_on_context_initialization=False`
  for the Gemini service in session mode.
- **T15.6** `voice/embodiment.py`: `bot_speaking`; runner `speaking`
  seam and `_wait_quiet` (4 s before a greeting; after a goodbye 3 s for
  it to start, then up to 10 s to finish);
  `CurrentLearner.walkaway` / `wish_recorded`; `wish_followup()` in the
  `save_session_notes` result when the booth persona is on
  (`build_tutor_tools(ask_wish=...)`).
- **T15.7** `voice/embodiment.py`: `note_user_stopped()` and the
  `turn: first sound Ns after the visitor stopped speaking` line; cloud
  mode feeds it from the collector's energy gate (`on_speech_end`).
- **T15.8** the identity protocol in `start_booth.sh`'s checklist and
  below; `tests/t15/`.
- **Found by the live seat-swap test, same evening:** (1)
  `enroll_new_learner` stored whatever face was in front of the camera
  *when the tool ran* -- in the test, Scott had taken the seat by the
  time the interview ended, so "Sunita" was enrolled with his face and
  he was then recognised as her at 1.000. `enrollment_face()` now
  stores the face that started the session whenever the capture
  disagrees with it (`session_face` seam on the enrollment tools).
  (2) The goodbye still merged into the next greeting: after the notes
  save the goodbye is not *playing* yet, it is being generated, so
  "not speaking" was not "done". `_wait_quiet` takes a `start_grace`
  (3 s for speech to begin, then up to 10 s for it to end).
- Also: `booth/next_visitor.sh` header no longer claims one visitor per
  launch; `tests/t14/test_one_visitor.py` updated to the four-miss rule.

## Protocol for the next family session (T15.8)

Run these in order, with the log open (`grep -E "session:|voice:|turn:"
voice/run.log`):

1. **A enrolls and says bye.** Expect `saved session notes`, then the
   wish question, then `wish recorded`, then the goodbye.
2. **B sits down without A leaving the room** (A steps out of frame).
   Expect a stranger greeting within ~3 s and no mention of A.
3. **A and B swap seats mid-lesson without a word.** Expect `a different
   face is in front of the robot` at once and `face swap` within 3 s,
   A's goodbye and notes, then B greeted as themselves (or as new).
4. **A returns after B's goodbye.** Expect `recognized a (score …)`,
   "welcome back" with A's notes, no enrollment questions.
5. **A speaks only the lesson language for a full minute.** Expect no
   `does not sound like themselves`; a `too short to judge` line is
   fine. If a challenge fires, note the scores: that is the
   calibration data T15.2 still needs.
6. **Lag.** After a few exchanges, `grep "turn: first sound"`; anything
   over ~2 s is worth a look at what the model did first (a `perform`
   before speaking shows in the lines just above).
