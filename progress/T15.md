## What changed (2026-09-04, evening)

- **T15.1** `voice/session.py`: the frame loop is
  `observe(face, now, frame_shape)`; mid-session the loop runs
  `recognize.analyze` every `face_recheck_secs` (2 s) — and at once
  after a face gap ≥ 1 s or a voice doubt — and `_check_identity`
  compares the embedding with the face that started the session
  (`_session_face`). Same face (≥ face ACCEPT 0.45): `_same_face_at`.
  Different face (< face REJECT 0.25) for `swap_secs` (3 s): `face
  swap` → `end_session` (walk-away cue: goodbye + notes) → the watching
  loop starts the newcomer's session from clean `_recent_vectors`.
  `speaker_changed()` now: face seen within `face_vouch_secs` (5 s) →
  ignore and `rearm()` the voice; a face present but unjudged → force a
  recheck and let the face decide; nobody in frame → end as before.
  `BOOTH_NOTE` on every runner prompt. Spec §A rewritten.
- **T15.2** `voice/voiceid.py`: `change_after=4`,
  `decision_min_secs=3.0` (short samples feed the print only; injected
  files are always judged, `secs=None`), `rearm()`, the real-voice
  numbers next to the thresholds. `VoiceCollector.on_speech_end`.
- **T15.3** `voice/tutor_mode.py`: `face_confirms()`;
  `confirm_identity` captures the current face (`current_face` seam,
  default two snapshots from the hub) and refuses below the face reject
  line, telling the model to ask their name and treat them as new;
  success re-arms the voice check.
- **T15.4** `voice/agent.py`: `VISION_FACE_LOOK` via `vision_text()`;
  `tutor_mode._BRIEFING` "Setting a task" rule with `task_language`
  (English below advanced).
- **T15.5** `voice/agent.py`: `inference_on_context_initialization=False`
  for the Gemini service in session mode.
- **T15.6** `voice/embodiment.py`: `bot_speaking`; runner `speaking`
  seam and `_wait_quiet` (4 s before a greeting; after a goodbye 3 s for
  it to start, then up to 10 s to finish);
  `CurrentLearner.walkaway` / `wish_recorded`; `wish_followup()` in the
  `save_session_notes` result when the booth persona is on
  (`build_tutor_tools(ask_wish=...)`).
- **T15.7** `voice/embodiment.py`: `note_user_stopped()` and the
  `turn: first sound Ns after the visitor stopped speaking` line; cloud
  mode feeds it from the collector's energy gate (`on_speech_end`).
- **T15.8** the identity protocol in `start_booth.sh`'s checklist and
  below; `tests/t15/`.
- **Found by the live seat-swap test, same evening:** (1)
  `enroll_new_learner` stored whatever face was in front of the camera
  *when the tool ran* -- in the test, Scott had taken the seat by the

- Also: `booth/next_visitor.sh` header no longer claims one visitor per
  launch; `tests/t14/test_one_visitor.py` updated to the four-miss rule.

## Seen live (2026-09-04 evening, the first booth run on T15) → T15.9

The run itself confirmed T15.5 and T15.7 on metal: one greeting at the
walk-up, no words to the empty chair, `turn: first sound` 1.3–2.7 s per
reply (one 4.6 s outlier where the model called enroll_new_learner
twice), the face recheck firing when Alex left and re-entered the frame
(`a face is back after 1.0s away, checking who`), and no voice challenge
in twenty minutes (every sample was 1.7–2.5 s: "too short to judge").
Then three faults, all in the log:

- **Head jerks, seen live 2026-09-04 20:08:40 -- fixed the same night.**
  Three accepted motion commands within 12 ms: an embodiment posture
  (pitch/antennas, 0.8 s), `play_move cheer` (the recorded 4 s bounce)
  and the model's `move_head yaw 10°` (0.6 s) -- the model called
  `perform` and `move_head` in one reply despite the "at most one
  movement per reply" rule, and the daemon accepted both because they
  arrived before the move had started (later postures during the move
  were rejected: "Ignoring set_target: a move is currently running").
  A recorded move drives absolute head poses; a goto_posture fighting
  its first keyframes is the jerk. Seven seconds earlier the tracker had
  held head yaw at 30-34° for 3 s (just under the 35° body handoff),
  so the dance started from a head cranked to the side. Candidates:
  (1) make `perform` exclusive -- drop or queue any other motion tool
  call in the same reply, and have the tools return one at a time;
  (2) go to neutral (0.5 s) before a recorded move starts, then play;
  (3) lower the handoff so the body takes over before the head is that
  far round. A second `move_head 10°` at 20:09:39 was alone and small.
- **Dances last one second, seen live 2026-09-04 20:11:24 (same root as
  the jerks) -- fixed the same night.** `perform dance` → `play_move dance repeat=3` (27 s)
  accepted; 0.85 s later the embodiment's talking sway sent an ordinary
  `goto_posture` (pitch/antennas), and `reachy_driver._begin` supersedes
  whatever is running ("a new gesture beats a stale one") -- it called
  `cancel_move` on the vendor and the dance stopped. Every recorded
  move today died the same way: the 18:54 and 20:08 cheers within
  ~1.4 s of embodiment sway, the 20:08 cheer even earlier (the model's
  `move_head` 8 ms after it -- the vendor's 0.4 s initial goto to the
  move's first frame, then an abort: that was the jerk). The stub plays
  a move in 0.2 s, so T14.5's "30 s dances" never saw this. Fix: while a
  `play_move` runs, embodiment sends nothing (suspend it like the
  tracker), and `_begin` should let only another play_move, `home` or a
  cancel supersede a recorded move -- not a sway or a tool nudge.
- **A goodbye nobody said, then "before you go" twice (20:11:27,
  20:13:36) -- fixed the same night.** The lesson topic was zai jian (goodbye); when Alex asked
  for a dance the model called `save_session_notes`, said "Go and
  practice. I will know if you did not", and the T15.6 notes result told
  it to ask the wish question. Alex pushed back; the model apologised
  but kept the pending instruction and tacked the wish question onto a
  normal lesson turn two minutes later, with no tool call. So T15.6
  amplifies a false goodbye. Fix candidates: (1) the wish follow-up only
  when the visitor's last words look like a farewell (the runner already
  sees `heard`/`said`; or ask the model to pass `farewell=true` to
  save_session_notes and gate on it); (2) in the briefing, a line that
  teaching the word goodbye is not the student leaving, and notes are
  saved only when the student says they are done; (3) let a pushed-back
  goodbye undo the "saved" state so a real one later still saves.

- **The base twitching at ~2 Hz with a tiny amplitude** (reported by
  the family during the run, not visible in the log: nothing samples
  the measured pose). Cause by reading: `reachy_target.goto` passed
  the *held* body_yaw with every goto, and the vendor's `goto_target`
  re-plans every group it is given from the present pose -- so each
  embodiment sway posture (every ~1.2 s) and each tracker pitch nudge
  re-drove the base servo from wherever it sat to the held target, and
  the two streams together approach 2 Hz. Measured with
  `tests/t15/probe_body_twitch.py` (below).

### What T15.9 changed

- `reachy_driver._begin`: a running `play_move` is refused to
  everything but `play_move`, `home`, `sleep`, `wake_up` (and
  `cancel_motion`); the nudge gets `accepted=False` with a reason, the
  move plays on. `robot_link._fire` logs refusals.
- `reachy_target.play_move`: `initial_goto_duration` 0.4 → 1.0 s.
- `reachy_target.goto`: head / antennas / body_yaw are passed to the
  vendor only when the caller named a DOF in that group; the others are
  None ("keep the current value").
- `voice/embodiment.py`: `hold(seconds)`; `_posture` sends nothing
  while holding. `voice/agent.py` `perform` holds the embodiment and
  suspends the tracker for the move plus two seconds, and `move_head`,
  `turn_body`, `nod`, `shake_head`, `wiggle_antennas` answer
  `skipped` with the reason while a move plays.
- `voice/tutor_mode.py`: `save_session_notes(farewell=...)`; the wish
  follow-up only with `farewell=true`, and it now says to drop the
  question if they are staying; a non-farewell save answers "carry
  on"; the briefing and the booth persona say that teaching the word
  goodbye or a dance request is not the student leaving, and never to
  ask the wish question twice.

### Base twitch probe (metal, 2026-09-04 20:21)

`tests/t15/probe_body_twitch.py`: measured body_yaw sampled at 15 Hz
for 8 s per regime, head/antenna gotos every 0.9 s as the embodiment
sends them. One encoder tick is 3.07 mrad (0.18°), which is the
resolution of everything below.

| regime | head | body_yaw in the goto | p2p | rms | dominant |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| idle | centred | – | 0.00 mrad | 0.00 | – |
| old (pre-T15.9) | centred | held value, every goto | 3.07 mrad | 0.42 | 0.4 Hz |
| new (T15.9) | centred | None | 0.00 mrad | 0.00 | – |
| old | yawed 30° | held value | 3.07 mrad | 1.36 | 0.1 Hz |
| new | yawed 30° | None | 3.07 mrad | 0.32 | 1.5 Hz |

So every head goto that carried the held body_yaw flipped the base by
one tick; without it the base is still with the head centred, and four
times quieter with the head turned 30° (where the vendor's head IK
itself moves the base target a little). The family's "~2 Hz" is the
embodiment's sway (0.9–1.5 s) plus the tracker's nudges (0.8 s)
interleaved; the probe drives one stream, hence 0.4 Hz. Not
reproduced: the amplitude the family saw, which a one-tick probe
cannot resolve -- if the base still visibly twitches with the head
turned, the remaining lever is the tracker's body handoff (turn the
base earlier, at 20°, so the head is never held that far round).

## Protocol for the next family session (T15.8)

Run these in order, with the log open (`grep -E "session:|voice:|turn:"
voice/run.log`):

1. **A enrolls and says bye.** Expect `saved session notes`, then the
   wish question, then `wish recorded`, then the goodbye.
2. **B sits down without A leaving the room** (A steps out of frame).
   Expect a stranger greeting within ~3 s and no mention of A.
3. **A and B swap seats mid-lesson without a word.** Expect `a different
   face is in front of the robot` at once and `face swap` within 3 s,
   A's goodbye and notes, then B greeted as themselves (or as new).
4. **A returns after B's goodbye.** Expect `recognized a (score …)`,
   "welcome back" with A's notes, no enrollment questions.
5. **A speaks only the lesson language for a full minute.** Expect no
   `does not sound like themselves`; a `too short to judge` line is
   fine. If a challenge fires, note the scores: that is the
   calibration data T15.2 still needs.
6. **Lag.** After a few exchanges, `grep "turn: first sound"`; anything
   over ~2 s is worth a look at what the model did first (a `perform`
   before speaking shows in the lines just above).
